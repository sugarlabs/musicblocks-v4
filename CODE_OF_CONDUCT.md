# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Music Blocks is built for learners, and it is largely built by them too. A good part of this
codebase began as somebody's first pull request. We pledge to keep participation in this
project free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that keep this community open, welcoming, diverse, and
healthy.

## Our Standards

Behavior that builds this community:

- Showing patience with beginners, and remembering that everyone here was one
- Giving feedback on the work rather than the person, and giving reasons alongside criticism
- Accepting feedback gracefully, and taking responsibility when you get something wrong
- Assuming good intent when a message reads ambiguously, and asking before escalating
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for the learners who use Music Blocks

Behavior that is unacceptable:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private, including sustained unwelcome contact
- Insults, personal attacks, and derogatory comments, including in code review
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, and inflammatory comments made to provoke a reaction
- Gatekeeping, belittling someone for what they do not yet know, or mocking questions
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission
- Dismissing or attacking requests for inclusion or accessibility
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code
review, discussions, and our chat channels. It also applies when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces, such as posting from an official account or acting
as an appointed representative at an event.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that breaks this Code of Conduct, report it to the
maintainers at `TODO: reporting email`. Reports can also be raised privately with any
maintainer directly.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obliged to
respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident. You do not need to be
certain that something was a violation to report it, and reporting in good faith will never
count against you.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take fair
corrective action in response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful. They have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will explain their
reasoning when they do.

Consequences scale with the behavior:

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional: a
   private, written warning explaining what was wrong and why. A public apology may be
   requested.
2. **Warning.** For a violation through a single incident or a series of actions: a warning
   with consequences for continued behavior, including a set period of no interaction with
   those involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation of community standards, or sustained
   inappropriate behavior: a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with
   the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violation, sustained harassment, or aggression toward
   individuals or groups: a permanent ban from any public interaction within the project.
